Privacy policy

Standinkey for Windows and Standinkey for Android · effective 6 October 2026

In short: Standinkey collects nothing about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. What you type, say and point at travels from your tablet to your PC over your own network, is used there, and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

Standinkey is made by Zen Republic, Spain. Contact: info@zenrepublic.es.

What the apps handle, and where it goes

WhatWhat happens to it
Keystrokes, text and mouse movements Sent from the tablet to the paired PC, which types and points with them. Not stored. The PC program's log records that a key or some text arrived, never what it was.
Microphone audio, while you dictate Recorded by the tablet only while dictation is switched on, streamed to your PC, and turned into text there by a speech model running on the PC itself. Not stored, and never sent to an online service.
Selected text, for spelling and layout fixes Copied on the PC through its clipboard, fixed on the PC, and pasted back; your clipboard is restored. The text does not leave the PC.
Device names, pairing keys and certificates Kept on the tablet and on the PC so the two recognise each other. Never sent anywhere else. Removing a device in either app deletes them.
Settings and logs Kept on each device. Logs exist to troubleshoot and contain no typed text.

Network connections

Between your tablet and PC: a connection on your local network, encrypted with TLS, accepted only from a tablet paired with a PIN shown on the PC; or Bluetooth, if you use the tablet as a Bluetooth keyboard.

To the internet, the apps only download files, and only these:

  • Language packs, from GitHub Pages (yuri-parfentcov.github.io), when a language is installed or updated.
  • The speech recognition model, from GitHub, the first time you dictate.
  • The spelling model, from Hugging Face, the first time you use the spelling fix.

Those services see the request, as any website does (your IP address and the file asked for), under their own privacy policies: GitHub, Hugging Face. Nothing about you or your typing is sent with it.

Permissions on the tablet

Microphone, for dictation. Bluetooth, to act as a Bluetooth keyboard. Network access, to reach your PC and download language packs. Notifications, to show that the keyboard is connected. Each is used only for that.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; everything the apps keep is on your own devices, and uninstalling them removes it. If you have questions under the GDPR or any other law, write to info@zenrepublic.es.

Children

Standinkey is a general-purpose tool, not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
