Standinkey

Your Android tablet as a keyboard and mouse for your Windows PC.

Standinkey turns a tablet into a full keyboard, a mouse pad and a microphone for the PC in front of you. It types in every language the PC has installed, knows the shortcuts of the program you are in, and lets you dictate — the PC does the listening, locally, with no cloud service.

Standinkey for Windows

The small program on the PC that the tablet talks to over your Wi-Fi. Free from the Microsoft Store.

Standinkey for Android

The keyboard itself. Works with the PC program over Wi-Fi, or as an ordinary Bluetooth keyboard with nothing installed.

In 24 languages

English, Deutsch, Español and Русский are built in; twenty more arrive as free language packs — both on the tablet and on the PC.

Private by design

No account, no advertising, no analytics. What you type goes from your tablet to your PC on your own network and nowhere else. Privacy policy
